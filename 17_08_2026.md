What I Suggest: The "Micro-Live" Strategy
Rather than jumping into live trading with full capital or staying stuck in paper trading, the industry standard is Micro-Live Trading.

TIP

What is Micro-Live? Fund a dedicated Binance account or sub-account with just $50 to $100 USDT, and configure the bot to trade with the minimum allowed order size ($10–$20 per trade).

Why this works: You get 100% authentic exchange fills, real slippage data, real fee tracking, and real execution logs, but if a trade hits its stop loss, you only lose $0.15 to $0.40.

2. Configure Micro-Sizing in config.py
Adjust 

config.py
 for small, safe testing:

python
# Enable live trading (or set DRY_RUN=false in environment)
DRY_RUN = False
# Trade only 1 symbol first (e.g. BTC/USDT or SOL/USDT)
SYMBOLS = ["BTC/USDT"]
# Restrict maximum concurrent positions
MAX_POSITIONS = 1
# Sizing floors (Binance minimum notional is $5-$10)
MIN_POSITION_USD = 15.0
RISK_PER_TRADE_PCT = 0.01      # Risk max 1% per trade
DAILY_LOSS_LIMIT_PCT = 0.03    # Hard kill-switch: stops bot if daily loss reaches 3%
